# Shopify Stock Visualization, Time-Series Analysis & Financial Insights

## Project Overview

This project analyzes **Shopify stock price data** using time-series analysis and data visualization.

The analysis focuses on stock prices, trading volume, daily returns, moving averages, and periods of high volatility.

## Objectives

* Analyze Shopify's daily stock prices.
* Plot 20-day and 50-day moving averages.
* Study OHLC prices and trading volume over time.
* Analyze the distribution of daily returns.
* Identify normal and fat-tailed return patterns.
* Find periods of high and low stock volatility.
* Create a financial summary of the stock performance.

## Visualizations

### 1. Moving Average Analysis

The **20-day and 50-day moving averages** are plotted together with the daily closing price.

Moving averages help identify the general direction of the stock price and reduce short-term price fluctuations.

### 2. OHLC Price Analysis

Line plots are created for:

* Open price
* High price
* Low price
* Closing price

These plots show how Shopify's stock price changed over time.

### 3. Trading Volume

Trading volume is plotted over time to understand how actively the stock was traded.

High trading volume can occur during periods of significant price movement or market activity.

### 4. Daily Returns

Daily returns are calculated to study the daily percentage change in the stock price.

A **histogram and KDE plot** are used to understand the distribution of daily returns.

The distribution is checked for:

* Normal distribution
* Fat-tailed distribution
* Extreme positive or negative returns

### 5. Volatility Analysis

Periods with large changes in daily returns are analyzed to identify **high-volatility periods**.

Periods with smaller price changes indicate relatively lower volatility.

## Financial Summary

The analysis provides information about:

* Overall stock price movement.
* Short-term and long-term price trends.
* Trading activity.
* Daily return behavior.
* High-volatility periods.
* General stock stability during the analyzed period.

## Key Insights

The analysis can help understand whether Shopify's stock experienced:

* Strong upward or downward price movements.
* Large short-term fluctuations.
* Changes in trading volume.
* Unusual or extreme daily returns.
* Periods of higher market volatility.

## Conclusion

This project uses **time-series analysis and financial visualization** to understand Shopify stock behavior.

Moving averages, OHLC prices, trading volume, daily returns, and volatility analysis provide a clear view of the stock's historical performance and risk patterns.

The results can be used for further financial analysis and research.
