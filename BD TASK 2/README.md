# TASK 2: RETAIL SALES VISUALIZATION & BUSINESS INSIGHTS

## PROJECT OVERVIEW

This project analyzes the **Superstore Sales dataset** using Python.
The analysis focuses on Sales, Profit, Discount, Quantity, and product categories.

## OBJECTIVES

* Analyze Sales and Profit.
* Compare sales and profit by category.
* Study the effect of Discount on Profit.
* Find relationships between numerical data.
* Identify outliers and important business insights.

## TECHNOLOGIES USED

* Python
* Google Colab
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn

## DATASET

**Dataset:** `SampleSuperstore.csv`

Main columns used:

* Sales
* Profit
* Quantity
* Discount
* Category
* Sub-Category

## KEY FINDINGS

* Technology has the highest sales and profit.
* Higher discounts are generally linked with lower profit.
* Sales and Profit have a moderate positive relationship.
* Some transactions have very high profits or losses.
* Quantity has only a weak relationship with Profit.
