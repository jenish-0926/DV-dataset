# Healthcare Data Visualization, Cost Relationship & Policy Insights

## Project Overview

This project analyzes healthcare data to understand **medical costs, patient admissions, medical conditions, and insurance providers**.

Different charts are used to make the data easy to understand and find useful patterns.

## Objectives

* Compare billing amounts for different medical conditions.
* Compare healthcare costs across insurance providers.
* Study the distribution of billing amounts.
* Analyze patient admissions over time.
* Identify possible seasonal changes in admissions.
* Understand the relationship between age, hospital stay duration, and medical cost.
* Provide useful recommendations for healthcare management.

## Visualizations

### 1. Stacked Bar Chart

A stacked bar chart is used to compare **billing amounts across medical conditions and insurance providers**.

It helps us understand which conditions and insurance providers are associated with higher billing amounts.

### 2. Violin Plot

Violin plots are used to study the **distribution of billing amounts**.

They help us see the range, spread, and common values of medical costs.

### 3. Temporal Line Chart

A line chart is used to show **patient admissions over time**.

It helps identify months or periods when the number of admissions increases or decreases.

This can help identify possible **seasonal admission patterns**.

### 4. Correlation Matrix

A correlation matrix is created using:

* Age
* Length of hospital stay
* Total medical cost

It helps understand whether these variables have a positive or negative relationship.

**Note:** Correlation shows a relationship between variables. It does not prove that one variable causes another.

## Key Insights

The visualizations can help healthcare organizations:

* Identify areas with higher medical costs.
* Understand billing patterns.
* Monitor changes in patient admissions.
* Plan resources during periods of high admissions.
* Study how patient age and hospital stay relate to healthcare costs.

## Healthcare Management Recommendations

Based on the patterns found in the data, healthcare managers can:

1. Monitor high-cost medical conditions.
2. Plan staff and hospital resources according to admission trends.
3. Review billing patterns regularly.
4. Use data to improve hospital resource planning.
5. Study longer hospital stays and their relationship with total costs.
6. Continue monitoring healthcare costs to support better decision-making.

## Conclusion

This project uses **data visualization and correlation analysis** to understand healthcare costs and patient admission patterns.

The results can help healthcare organizations make **data-based decisions**, improve resource planning, and better understand medical expenses.
